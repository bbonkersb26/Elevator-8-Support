Privacy policy

Elevator 8 is provided by Brian Baker. This policy covers the game and this support website.

Game progress stays on your device

The game stores campaign progress, daily results, cosmetic Marks, settings and a reward transaction history locally. No game account is required. We do not operate a gameplay cloud-save service or upload your level decisions to our own server. Operating-system backups may include local files according to your device settings.

Optional advertising and consent

The iOS game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for optional rewarded ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform for consent and privacy choices. Ads do not unlock levels. You can disable optional ads in Settings without losing earned rewards. All levels and retries remain playable offline.

We configure non-personalized requests, disable publisher first-party IDs and publisher ad personalization, and limit ad content to Google’s G rating. We do not request Apple’s tracking permission or intentionally pass your name, email, gameplay progress or an account ID to the advertising SDK. These settings do not mean the providers collect no information.

Google’s SDKs may process the following information to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, maintain service quality and manage privacy choices:

InformationExamples and purposes
Approximate locationLocation inferred from IP address for contextual advertising, reporting and consent requirements.
Device identifiersIdentifiers available under device permissions for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention.
Advertising and interaction dataAds shown, interactions and SDK usage for advertising, analytics and consent functionality.
DiagnosticsCrash, performance and other diagnostic information for reliability, analytics and service operation.

Some provider data may be associated with device identifiers. Google determines its retention periods and processes information under its own policies. See Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.

Your choices

Where required, consent or opt-out messages explain available choices before ads can be requested. A Privacy choices control is available in Settings when the consent provider requires it. Turning Optional ads off prevents new ad initialization and loading by the game; the consent service may still refresh privacy information at launch. Previously collected provider information is not deleted by this switch.

The game does not request location permission, collect your date of birth or use a developer-operated analytics service. We do not intentionally collect children’s personal information. The game contains unsettling themes and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support request, contact us.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request and investigate problems. Do not send sensitive information. We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle support, maintain relevant records or meet applicable obligations. Contact us to request deletion.

Deletion and privacy requests

Removing the app can delete its local game files; operating-system backups may remain until you remove them. The developer cannot access or restore those local files remotely. To ask about information you sent directly to us, email relativityrush@gmail.com. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information or object to certain processing. For information controlled by Google, use the controls and contacts in Google’s privacy policy.

This website

This static support site does not include advertising, analytics scripts, sign-in forms or a contact form. The hosting service may process basic request information such as an IP address and browser details to deliver and secure the website. Email links open your chosen email app; sending a message is your choice.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the game’s data practices change. The effective date appears above.
