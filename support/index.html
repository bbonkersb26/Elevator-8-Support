How do I clear a shift?

Inspect Landing, Monitor and Cabin. Reference shows the matching arrangement for this exact inspection. OPEN admits a normal landing; GO certifies a normal cabin. HOLD responds to a change. Missing a change ends the shift. One mistaken HOLD is allowed; a second ends it.

Why are the decision buttons waiting?

Spend a short moment looking at all three views. The observation gate prevents accidental decisions before you have seen the scene. Return from Reference to make your choice. There is no countdown.

What is a clean clear?

Eight correct decisions without a continuation earn a clean badge. Assisted clears still unlock the next level, but do not earn a clean badge or the clean first-clear bonus. Replaying a level is always free.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress is stored on this device. Pause or Home keeps an unfinished shift available. There is no game account or developer-operated cloud save. Deleting the app can remove its progress. Device backups are controlled by your operating system.

How do daily shifts work?

The daily changes at midnight UTC. One first attempt is recorded for each date, followed by unlimited free practice. Practice earns no Marks. A fixed set of 365 daily decks rotates annually.

How do optional rewards work?

After the first three campaign runs, eligible results may offer an ad. A continuation requires at least two correct decisions and viewing the failed incident. Completing the ad saves an entitlement; tap Resume to use it. A Marks bonus matches the positive base award already saved for that result. Free retry, Next and Home remain available.

Closing an ad early does not earn a reward. Keep the app open until reward saving finishes. If the app closes before the provider confirms the reward, it may not be recoverable. A saved continuation for a replaced shift becomes one credit for a later eligible campaign failure.

No ad is available, or a reward is still saving

Availability varies by connection, consent, region and provider inventory. Ad limits also apply. Continue playing for free; there is no need to keep trying. A “Reward save pending” notice means the app is retrying a local save. Keep it open and make sure the device has free storage. Contact support if the notice persists.

How do I disable ads or change privacy choices?

In Settings, switch Optional ads off. Earned rewards remain yours. Where required, the Privacy choices button opens the provider’s consent settings. Consent checks may still connect to the provider at app launch. See the privacy policy for details.

Report an inappropriate or broken ad

Email support with the approximate time, device model and a description of the ad. Include a screenshot only if you are comfortable sharing it. Please avoid clicking the ad to investigate it, and omit personal information.

Sound, motion and readability

Settings offers Sound, Quiet transitions, Large text and Scene descriptions. The clues do not require sound, fast reactions or a timer. Pause whenever needed.
